How acreage mode works

Start Walking requests GPS access and records your path automatically. Add Point lets you manually save your current GPS position at important corners. Finish closes the polygon from your last recorded point back to your first and calculates the enclosed area.

Phone GPS is useful for locating and estimating acreage, but it is not a legal land survey. Tree cover, terrain, buildings, and satellite geometry can move individual readings by several feet or more.

On iPhone, browser GPS normally requires this page to be served from an HTTPS website. Opening the HTML directly as a local file:// may not allow location access.

How coordinate finder works

The app continuously calculates the straight-line distance and true bearing from your current GPS position to the entered coordinate.

The pointer combines that bearing with the iPhone compass heading. If compass access is unavailable, the app still shows distance and bearing, but the pointer may remain referenced to north instead of the direction the phone is facing.
